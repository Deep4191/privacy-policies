Privacy Policy

Loan EMI Calculator is a fully offline tool designed to help users calculate monthly loan installments and totals. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

1. Data Collection

This app does not collect any personal or financial data. All inputs (loan amount, interest rate, tenure) remain on your device and are used only for real-time calculations.

2. No Internet Usage

This calculator operates entirely offline. No internet connection is required, and no data is transmitted to any external server or third party.

3. No Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

4. Children's Privacy

This app does not target children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

If there are any updates to this policy, they will be posted on this page.
